Meal Buffet — REFramework mod for Monster Hunter Wilds
====================================================

What it does
------------
Applies any canteen meal buff to your own hunter on demand, without going to
the canteen and without spending a Meal Voucher. Meal names come from the
game's own master data at runtime, nothing is hardcoded.

The mod is in development. It writes meal state on your local hunter only; it
never touches save data and never touches other players.

Install
-------
1. Install REFramework for MH Wilds (dinput8.dll in the game folder).
2. Copy meal_buffet.lua to:
     <game>\reframework\autorun\meal_buffet.lua
   Steam path:
     ...\steamapps\common\MonsterHunterWilds\reframework\autorun\
3. Open the in-game REFramework menu -> "Script Generated UI" ->
   "Meal Buffet" to confirm it loaded.

Use
---
1. Press Insert to open the REFramework menu.
2. Script Generated UI -> Meal Buffet.
3. Pick a base meal from the list.
4. Pick the extra meal skills, or reroll them, until you like the set.
5. Press "Apply" to put the meal on your hunter.

Works in the hub, at camp, and mid-quest.

Notes
-----
- No voucher and no ingredients are consumed.
- Meal list and skill pool come from the game's master data at runtime.
- Reload via REFramework menu -> "Script" -> "Reset scripts".
- If a game patch renames an internal meal field, the mod logs a per-field
  miss instead of erroring.
