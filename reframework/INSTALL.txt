Meal Buffet — REFramework mod for Monster Hunter Wilds
====================================================

Status: v0.2.0. Meals and consumables verified in the hub and in quests.

What it does
------------
Applies any canteen meal, and any consumable buff, to your own hunter on demand
without going to the canteen and without spending a Meal Voucher, ingredients
or items.

Meals: pick a meal set, choose or reroll its two extra buffs, press Apply.
Clear meal removes the active meal (the Meal Stripper behaviour), and applying a
new meal replaces the active one directly, so you never need to strip first.

Consumables: drugs, seeds, pills, powders and drinks (Demondrug, Armorskin,
Might/Adamant Seed, Might/Adamant Pill, Demon/Hardshell Powder, Dash Juice,
Immunizer, Hot Drink, Cool Drink). Tick the ones you want and press Apply
selected, use the Standard preset, or save your own sets in the four slots.
Nothing is consumed and nothing has to be in your pouch. Item Prolonger can be
toggled on/off and overridden by level.

Auto toggles (one shot per quest):
- Auto preset on quest start: applies the selected preset when a quest begins.
- Auto Hot/Cool Drink on quest start: one drink chosen from the locale.
    ST103 Oilwell Basin      -> Cool Drink
    ST104 Iceshard Cliffs    -> Hot Drink
    ST402 Omega Planetes     -> Hot Drink
    anything else            -> no drink

Meals, fixed skills, buff durations and item buffs come from the game itself at
runtime, nothing is hardcoded except the locale-to-drink table.

It writes state on your local hunter only: never other players, never save data,
no game files modified, no network.

Install
-------
1. Install REFramework for MH Wilds (dinput8.dll in the game folder).
2. Copy meal_buffet.lua to:
     <game>\reframework\autorun\meal_buffet.lua
   Steam path:
     ...\steamapps\common\MonsterHunterWilds\reframework\autorun\
3. Open the in-game REFramework menu -> "Script Generated UI" ->
   "Meal Buffet" to confirm it loaded.

Use
---
1. Press Insert -> Script Generated UI -> Meal Buffet.
2. Choose Meals or Consumables at the top of the panel.

Meals:
   - < Prev / Next > pick a meal set (base stats and fixed skills are listed).
   - For each extra buff slot, < / > cycles game roll -> none -> a specific
     buff. Picked buffs show a short description.
   - Press "Apply meal"; the active skills list shows what landed.
   - Press "Clear meal" to remove the active meal.

Consumables:
   - Tick buffs, then "Apply selected", or press "Apply" on a single row.
   - "Standard" applies the built-in set; "Save selection" stores your ticks in
     one of the four unnamed slots.
   - "Item Prolonger" toggles the duration bonus; < > overrides the level.
   - The auto toggles apply a preset or the map's Hot/Cool Drink at quest start.

Works in the hub, at camp, and during a quest.

Notes
-----
- No voucher, no ingredients and no items are consumed.
- Selections, presets and toggles live in reframework/data/meal_buffet.json.
- No hooks; the auto toggles poll the quest state a few times a second.
- Writes happen only when you press an Apply/Clear button or a toggle fires.
- Reload via REFramework menu -> "Script" -> "Reset scripts".
- If a game patch renames an internal field or method, the panel reports the
  failing call instead of throwing.
