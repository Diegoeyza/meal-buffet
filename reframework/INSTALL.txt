Meal Buffet — REFramework mod for Monster Hunter Wilds
====================================================

Status: v0.1.1. Verified in the hub, at camp and during a quest.

What it does
------------
Applies any canteen meal to your own hunter on demand, without going to the
canteen and without spending a Meal Voucher or ingredients. Pick a meal set,
choose or reroll its two extra buffs, press Apply.

Clear meal removes the active meal (the Meal Stripper behaviour), and applying
a new meal replaces the active one directly, so you never need to strip first.

Meals, fixed skills and the buff pool come from the game's master data at
runtime, nothing is hardcoded.

It writes meal state on your local hunter only: never other players, never save
data, no game files modified, no network.

Install
-------
1. Install REFramework for MH Wilds (dinput8.dll in the game folder).
2. Copy meal_buffet.lua to:
     <game>\reframework\autorun\meal_buffet.lua
   Steam path:
     ...\steamapps\common\MonsterHunterWilds\reframework\autorun\
3. Open the in-game REFramework menu -> "Script Generated UI" ->
   "Meal Buffet" to confirm it loaded.

Use
---
1. Press Insert -> Script Generated UI -> Meal Buffet.
2. < Prev / Next > pick a meal set (base stats and fixed skills are listed).
3. For each extra buff slot, < / > cycles game roll -> none -> a specific
   buff. Picked buffs show a short description.
4. Press "Apply meal"; the active skills list shows what landed.
5. Press "Clear meal" to remove the active meal.

Works in the hub, at camp, and during a quest.

Notes
-----
- No voucher and no ingredients are consumed.
- The selection is saved to reframework/data/meal_buffet.json.
- No hooks and no per-frame work; writes only when you press Apply or Clear.
- Reload via REFramework menu -> "Script" -> "Reset scripts".
- If a game patch renames an internal field or method, the panel reports the
  failing call instead of throwing.
